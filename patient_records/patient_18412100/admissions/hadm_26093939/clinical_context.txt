    PROCEDURE DETAILS:
    
    Following explanation of the risks, benefits and alternatives to the
    procedure, written informed consent was obtained from the patient. The patient
    was then brought to the computed tomography suite and placed supine on the
    imaging table.  General anesthesia was induced by the anesthesiologist. 
    Following scout imaging, the skin was marked and draped in the usual sterile
    fashion.
    
    Under continuous ultrasound guidance, a 3.8 mm cryoablation probe was advanced
    into the residual desmoid tumor.  Subsequently a 2.4 mm cryoablation probe was
    advanced parallel to the first probe within the tumor.  Subsequently, at a
    more inferior location, two 3.8 mm cryoablation probes were placed in
    parallel.  Cryoablation with these four probes was performed with freezing for
    15 minutes followed by 10 minutes of thawing and then 15 minutes of freezing. 
    Non-contrast CT was performed during the initial freezing at 5, 10 and 15
    minutes to assess the ablation zone.
    
    Subsequently, a slightly superior and medial area of residual tumor was
    targeted for cryoablation.  3.8 mm and 2.4 mm probes were advanced into
    residual tumor under CT fluoroscopic guidance.  Cryoablation was performed
    with freezing for 6 minutes, thawing 4 minutes, and freezing for 6 minutes. 
    Noncontrast CT was performed during the initial freezing at 5 minutes to
    assess the ablation zone.
    
    The cryoablation probes were removed and hemostasis was achieved.  The skin
    was then cleaned and dry sterile dressings were applied. The patient was
    awakened from general anesthesia without incident and there were no immediate
    post-procedure complications. The patient was transferred to the
    post-anesthesia care unit for further monitoring.
